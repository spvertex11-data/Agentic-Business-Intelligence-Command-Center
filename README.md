# Agentic Business Intelligence Command Center

### AI-Powered Business Analytics & Decision Support System

An end-to-end Business Intelligence portfolio project using **Python, PostgreSQL, Advanced SQL, Excel, Power BI, Gemini AI, and Agentic AI** to monitor business performance, investigate root causes, validate insights, and generate data-backed management recommendations.

---

## Project Objective

Traditional dashboards mainly answer:

> **What happened?**

This project goes further and helps answer:

> **Why did it happen?**  
> **Where is the problem?**  
> **What should management investigate next?**

The complete workflow is:

**Monitor → Detect → Investigate → Validate → Explain → Recommend**

---

## Business Problem

Management needs an analytics solution that not only tracks KPIs such as revenue, profit, margin, and orders but also helps investigate performance problems.

The system was designed to:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Detect revenue and profitability issues
- Identify loss-making products and transactions
- Analyze discount and shipping impact
- Perform root-cause investigation
- Provide evidence-based management recommendations

## Dataset

The project uses a global e-commerce sales dataset containing:

- 10,000 orders
- Data from 2021 to 2024
- 4 regions
- 18 countries
- 5 product categories
- 70 products

The dataset contains information about:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Discounts
- Product categories
- Customer segments
- Regions and countries
- Shipping cost
- Shipping method
- Order status
